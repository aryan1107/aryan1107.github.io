About Interior AI.

Interior AI is for anyone staring at a room and wondering what it could be. Take a photo from the doorway, choose a direction — Scandi, Japandi, Mid-Century, Boho, Industrial, Coastal, Minimal, Modern — and the app renders that room in that style while preserving its real architecture. It works from floor plans too.

Renders are produced by online AI services and count against a monthly allowance. The free plan includes a handful of renders; Premium raises the limit, adds more variants per run, and unlocks the design assistant.

What's inside.

  • Photo-to-render redesigns that keep walls, windows, and perspective intact.
  • Before / after comparison, multiple variants, and word-driven refinements.
  • Mask editing to change one area — the sofa, the wall colour — and nothing else.
  • Floor-plan mode: sketch or import a plan and see it as a furnished room.
  • A design assistant (Premium) you can chat with, including about your own photos.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Siri shortcuts, and Spotlight search for your designs.
