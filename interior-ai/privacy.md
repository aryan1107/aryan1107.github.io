# Privacy Policy — Interior AI

This explains what information **Interior AI** ("the app") handles, how
it is used, and what choices you have. Plain English where possible. If
anything is unclear, see the [support page](support.html) for ways to
reach us.

Interior AI is offered with a free plan and an optional Premium
subscription. By using Interior AI you agree to the practices described
here.

## Summary

Interior AI turns a photo of a room (or a floor plan) into a redesigned
render, lets you refine that render, and — on Premium — lets you chat
with an AI design assistant, including about your own photos. Rendering
and chat need online AI processing, so the photos and messages you
choose to send leave your device for that purpose. We collect only what
is needed to run the app, keep usage limits honest, process purchases,
and let you find your work again.

## What we collect

- **Account identity** — you sign in with Google or Apple. We store the
  account identifier and email address the provider gives us. This is
  how your designs, chats, and plan status follow you across devices.
- **Room photos and floor plans** — images you take, pick from your
  library, or draw in the app are uploaded only when you start a
  render, a refinement, or attach them to a chat message.
- **Generated renders and masks** — the images the app produces for you,
  and any masks you paint, are stored so you can revisit, compare, and
  refine them later.
- **Prompts and refinements** — the room type, style, and any text you
  type to refine a render are sent for AI processing and saved with
  the project.
- **Chat messages** — conversations with the design assistant (text and
  attached photos) are stored so you can continue them later.
- **Profile preferences** — your first name (optional), the rooms you
  care about, and preferred styles, if you set them during onboarding.
- **Usage counters** — how many renders you have used against your
  monthly allowance, and a daily message counter for chat.
- **Purchase status** — whether a Premium entitlement is active, via
  our subscription provider. We never see your payment details; Apple
  handles those.
- **Diagnostics you send us** — if you email support from the app, the
  message includes your user ID, app version, build, device model, OS
  version, and locale so we can help faster. You can remove any of it.

We do **not** collect your contacts, precise location, or advertising
identifiers, and we do not sell personal data.

## How we use it

- To produce renders and chat replies you ask for.
- To keep your projects and chats available across your devices.
- To enforce plan limits fairly and prevent abuse.
- To verify and restore purchases.
- To respond to support requests.
- To keep the service reliable and secure.

## Third-party services

Interior AI relies on a small number of providers. Each receives only
what is needed for its job:

- **Firebase (Google)** — sign-in, database, and file storage for your
  account, projects, chats, and images.
- **OpenAI** — receives your room photo, mask (if any), and prompt to
  generate and refine renders.
- **Replicate (running Google's Gemini model)** — receives your chat
  messages, recent conversation context, and attached photos to
  generate assistant replies.
- **RevenueCat** — verifies App Store subscriptions and tells the app
  whether Premium is active.
- **Apple** — processes payments, and provides Sign in with Apple.

These providers process data under their own policies. We do not
allow them to use your content to market to you.

## On-device features

Widgets, Spotlight search entries, Siri shortcuts, and Home Screen
quick actions are generated on your device from data already in the
app. Widget thumbnails are cached locally on your device only.

## Where your data lives and for how long

Your account data is stored in Google Cloud (Firebase) for as long as
your account exists. Uploaded photos and renders are kept so your
project history works; you can delete individual projects in the app.

## Deleting your account

Open **Settings → Account → Delete account**. After you confirm, the app
signs you out immediately and deletion continues on our servers even if
you close the app. We remove your projects, uploaded photos, renders,
masks, chats, and profile preferences, then delete your sign-in
account.

Two things are intentionally kept:

- **Render usage counters** for the deleted account, so that deleting
  and re-creating an account cannot be used to reset the free
  allowance. We also keep a copy of those counters keyed by a one-way
  hash of your email address for the same reason. This contains no
  photos, chats, or profile data.
- **Purchase records** required by our subscription provider and
  Apple for refunds and fraud prevention.

## Your choices

- You can decline camera and photo access; the app will then only work
  with images you explicitly pick.
- You can delete any project or chat at any time in the app.
- You can turn haptics and notifications off in Settings.
- You can ask us to export or delete your data via the
  [support page](support.html).

## Children

Interior AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the effective date
