# Support — Interior AI

Need a hand? You're in the right place. There are two ways to reach
us: a one-tap **Help & support** button inside the app, or a regular
email.

## The fastest way — in the app

Open Interior AI, then go to **Settings → Account → Help & support**
(or **Leave feedback**). It opens a pre-filled email that includes
your user ID, app version, build, device, and OS — which means we can
usually find and fix the problem on the first reply. Please leave that
footer in place.

If you can, please add:

- A short description of what you were trying to do.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if it's a visual issue.
- The room type and style involved, if the issue is about a render.

## Email us directly

Prefer plain email, or the app won't open? You can reach us at the
address below. Tap or copy it — it's a real working email, written
with brackets so automated bots don't scrape it:

> [**app+interiorai \[at\] brutusai \[dot\] com**](mailto:app+interiorai@brutusai.com)

We read every message and usually reply within one business day.

## Common questions

### My render looks nothing like my room

Renders keep the room's architecture from the photo you give them, so
the photo matters most. Shoot in daylight, from the doorway, as wide
as you can, and clear obvious clutter. Then use **Refine** to steer
("warmer palette", "keep the bookshelf") or **Edit area** to change one
spot only.

### How many renders do I get?

The free plan includes a small monthly allowance, shown on the Home
screen. Premium raises it substantially. A render that fails is never
charged against you.

### Why is Chat locked?

The design assistant is part of Premium. While your subscription is
active you can use it freely (a generous daily ceiling exists to
prevent abuse).

### How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store. Manage or cancel in
**Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions** on iPhone/iPad, or **System
Settings → Apple ID → Media & Purchases → Manage** on Mac. Cancellations
take effect at the end of the current billing period.

### I bought Premium but the app still says Free

Open the paywall and tap **Restore**. If that doesn't help, email us
with the in-app button so the message carries your user ID.

### How do I delete my account?

**Settings → Account → Delete account**, then confirm. You are signed
out right away and deletion finishes on our servers even if you close
the app. Your designs, photos, chats, and profile are removed. Render
usage counters are kept so the free allowance can't be reset by
re-creating an account — see the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

### Where are my designs?

Everything you generate lands in **Library**, and recent designs appear
on Home, in the Home Screen widget, and in Spotlight search.
